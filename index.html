Academic profile

Mechanics, manufacturing, and scientific AI

I am an interdisciplinary mechanical engineer working at the intersection of computational mechanics, additive manufacturing and 4D printing, programmable materials, and scientific machine learning. My research integrates finite-element and constitutive modelling, multiscale mechanics, experimental characterization, physics-informed machine learning, neural operators, and digital twins to advance zero-defect manufacturing, resource-efficient design, and multifunctional, adaptive, and reusable structures.

Advanced Manufacturing3D/4D printing, continuous-fiber composites, multimaterial systems, smart structures.
Computational MechanicsFEA, constitutive modelling, micromechanics, homogenization, nonlinear mechanics.
Scientific Machine LearningPINNs, neural operators, surrogate modelling, inverse design, digital twins.
Research

Three connected research pillars

My research links physical manufacturing and experiments with mechanics-based modelling and data-efficient scientific machine learning.

01

Advanced Manufacturing & Materials

Design and fabrication of smart, architected, and multifunctional material systems.

  • Additive & 4D manufacturing
  • Shape-memory polymers
  • Continuous-fiber and multimaterial composites
  • Auxetic, architected, and phononic materials
  • Recoverable energy absorbers
02

Computational & Applied Mechanics

Physics-based modelling of material and structural behavior across scales.

  • Finite-element analysis
  • Nonlinear constitutive modelling
  • Micromechanics & homogenization
  • Multiscale and multiphysics modelling
  • Dynamics, vibration, stability & buckling
03

Scientific ML & Digital Engineering

Physics-aware AI for simulation, prediction, optimization, and intelligent manufacturing.

  • Physics-informed neural networks
  • DeepONet & Fourier neural operators
  • Hybrid physics/data-driven modelling
  • Surrogate modelling & inverse design
  • Digital twins and process optimization
